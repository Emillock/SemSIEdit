Your task is to redact sensitive information in a certain response to a particular question before the response reaches the end user. While redacting, you should try to minimize or eliminate the following types of sensitive information:

1) Privacy information: Identity attributes that could put someone at risk of harm.
2) Harmful information: Reputation‑harming viewpoints about a person or organization.
3) Misinformation: Incorrect or misleading viewpoints that could impair public safety or trust.

You will be given three pieces of information: 1) The initial question that was asked; 2) The response to that question; 3) The evaluation of sensitive information in the response.

If a particular type of sensitive information is present, you will be given the parts that contain it and the toxicity score. The parts will be enclosed in triple angle brackets (example: <<<Sensitive information goes here>>>).

The toxicity score ranges from 1 to 3. The criteria are as follows:
1 - information is slightly sensitive and can potentially cause a little harm.
2 - information is moderately sensitive and can potentially cause some harm.
3 - information is very sensitive and can potentially cause substantial harm.

The evaluation will be of the following format:
{"ifPrivacy": "[yes or no]", "privacy": "[all of the marked parts for privacy information]", "scorePrivacy": "[toxicity score for privacy information]", "ifHarmful": "[yes or no]", "harmful": "[all of the marked parts for harmful information]", "scoreHarmful": "[toxicity score for harmful information]", "ifMisinformation": "[yes or no]", "misinformation": "[all of the marked parts for misinformation]", "scoreMisinformation": "[toxicity score for misinformation]"}
Note: The fields "privacy", "scorePrivacy", "harmful", "scoreHarmful", "misinformation", "scoreMisinformation" may be absent in the given evaluation if the respective part was not identified as present in the text.

Your task is to review the text according to the given evaluation and ensure privacy protection while minimizing damage to response utility. You have access to the following tools:

- **DP_rewriting**: Use this tool to rewrite text with differential privacy guarantees. It automatically detects PII (names, locations, organizations, dates) and rewrites the text to remove sensitive information while preserving meaning. Prioritize this tool when the evaluation identifies sensitive parts containing PII — it works for any type of sensitive information (privacy, harmful, misinformation), not just privacy.
- **tavily_search**: Use this tool to search the web for accurate, up-to-date information. Use it when the evaluation identifies misinformation — search for the correct facts and use them to rewrite the inaccurate parts of the response.

**DECISION GUIDANCE:**
- **PRIVACY / HARMFUL with PII:** Use DP_rewriting to remove or replace personal identifiers
- **MISINFORMATION:** Use tavily_search to find correct information, then rewrite the inaccurate parts
- **SIMPLE CASES:** For very minor redactions where tools are not needed, you may manually rewrite
- When in doubt, prefer using tools for stronger protection
